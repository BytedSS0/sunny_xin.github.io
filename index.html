I am a researcher on the ByteDance Seed team, focused on coding agents and the reinforcement-learning post-training that drives them. My work centers on software-engineering agents, long-horizon agents, and agent harnesses and sandbox infrastructure.

Current Research

Long-horizon coding agents: data synthesis and reinforcement-learning optimization for tasks including, but not limited to, ProgramBench, DeepSWE, PaperBench, and NL2Repo, alongside data synthesis for real-world, long-horizon workflows beyond benchmarks.
